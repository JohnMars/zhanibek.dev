Work

Bizum launched end-to-end earlier this year: P2P by phone-number alias, Request-to-Pay, merchant payments. A surprisingly complete instant-payments surface to ship from scratch.

Alongside the team work, I've been running the Maestro E2E testing strategy across business-critical flows that Espresso couldn't reach due to third-party SDKs. That work became the Droidcon Berlin 2025 talk. Also wiring OpenTelemetry tracing and Grafana dashboards into our checking-account surface - getting visibility into what actually happens in production matters more than I used to think.
